How to start

Download Solis.exe

Put it somewhere you'll find it, like Documents or the desktop. Your planner is saved in a data folder next to it.

Open it

Windows may show a blue warning the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway (see below).

Sign up

At Westbourne? Use your school email and open the link we send you. Not at Westbourne? Pick Just the planner.

Add your classes, start a timer

Then join your house and start building your island. Add friends by name and send them your invite link.

The blue "Windows protected your PC" screen

Solis is new, so Windows doesn't recognise it yet and shows this once. It's safe to open:

  1. Click More info (under the text).
  2. Click Run anyway (it appears bottom right).

If your laptop doesn't show Run anyway, ask your parent or IT to allow it, or use Solis on another computer.
