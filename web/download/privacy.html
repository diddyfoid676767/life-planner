Privacy, and info for parents and teachers

Plain English, no small print. Last updated November 2026.

What Solis is

A free study and life planner for Windows, built by a student. It keeps to-dos, classes, SACs and tests, a study timer, workouts and a calendar in one place, with a light game layer (an island you build with points from studying and exercise, and school houses that work together on a weekly "boss"). There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your computer

  • Your whole planner: tasks, classes, timetable, SACs and scores, study sessions, workouts, notes and calendar links.
  • It's saved in the data folder next to Solis.exe, with automatic backups.
  • If you add your own email or Claude API key for the optional email sorting, those stay on your computer too.

What goes online, only if you make an account

  • Your email and a password (stored by Google Firebase, which we use for sign-in). We email you a link once to confirm the address.
  • For Westbourne students: your name from the school's year list, your house, and that you're a verified student. The year list itself is never stored in readable form: only a scrambled (salted hash) version, used to check that an address is on it.
  • What friends see: your name, avatar, badges and leaderboard numbers (like study minutes this week), and your house's weekly totals.
  • A copy of your planner, private to you, so it can sync between your devices.
  • Chats you send to friends, and feedback you send to the developer.

What we never do

  • Sell or share your data with anyone. No ads, no tracking, no analytics companies.
  • Show your email to other students. Friends can find you by your name, or by typing your exact school email, but they never see the address.

Deleting your account

In Solis, open Settings, Account and press Delete account. Your profile, synced planner and username are removed from the server straight away. Your planner on your own computer stays until you delete the Solis folder.

Questions

Use the 💬 Feedback button inside Solis, which goes straight to the developer.
